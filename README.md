# Gemini ABC Piano Roll

A browser extension for chatting about music with **Google Gemini** and **NotebookLM**. 

It turns musical notes generated by the AI into an interactive piano roll right inside your chat, and gives you a built-in piano roll to draw and send your own melodies.

---

## What is this for?

Large language models like Gemini think and communicate in text. When you ask Gemini to write a melody or chord progression, it writes it in **ABC notation** — a text-based format for sheet music and melodies.

This extension bridges that gap:
- **Hear the music instantly**: Play generated melodies directly in the chat with real acoustic piano sounds.
- **Visual Piano Roll**: See the notes, chords, and rhythm visually instead of reading raw code.
- **Draw your own notes**: Open the built-in piano roll to compose chords or melodies and send them to Gemini with one click.
- **Export & Import MIDI**: Download any idea as a standard `.mid` file to open in your music software (DAW), or load your own MIDI files to ask Gemini for accompaniment, variations, or theory advice.

## Quick Installation

Works with Google Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge, Opera, and any Chromium browser:

1. Download or clone this repository to your computer.
2. Open your browser and go to `chrome://extensions`.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (toggle in the top-right corner).
4. Click **Load unpacked** (top-left) and select this folder.
5. Open Gemini or NotebookLM, ask for music, and enjoy!

## Shortcuts

- **Space**: Play / Pause
- **Scroll over piano keys**: Zoom key height
- **Scroll over timeline ruler**: Zoom time in / out
- **Left click**: Add / move note
- **Right click on note**: Delete note
- **Drag note edge**: Resize note length
